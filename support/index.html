Frequently asked questions

The game crashes or won’t open. What can I do?

First make sure the game is updated to the latest version from the App Store or Google Play and that your device has free storage. Restarting your device fixes most issues. If the problem continues, email us with your device model and OS version and we will look into it.

I made a purchase but did not receive it.

Purchases are processed by Apple or Google. Please wait a few minutes and restart the game; if the item still has not arrived, use the “Restore Purchases” option in the game settings if available. For refunds, contact Apple or Google directly through your store account, as we are not able to process refunds ourselves.

How do I remove ads or limit ad tracking?

Some of our games offer an in-app purchase to remove ads. To limit personalised advertising, change your device settings: on iOS go to Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking, and on Android go to Settings → Google → Ads.

How can I request deletion of my data?

Email us at hello@goodenoughgames.com with the subject “Data deletion request” and the name of the game. See the Request User Data Deletion section of our Privacy Policy for details, including how to contact our third-party partners.

I found a bug or have a suggestion.

We would love to hear it. Email us with as much detail as you can, including the steps to reproduce the bug. Suggestions for new features and games are always welcome.
