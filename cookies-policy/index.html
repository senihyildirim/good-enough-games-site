Cookies Policy

Last updated: October 8, 2026

This Cookies Policy explains how Good Enough Games (“we”, “us” or “our”) uses cookies and similar technologies on our website, good-enough.games, and in our mobile games.

What are cookies?

Cookies are small text files that a website stores on your device when you visit it. Similar technologies include local storage, pixels and, in mobile apps, device identifiers such as the advertising identifier (IDFA on iOS, Advertising ID on Android) and SDK-generated identifiers.

Cookies on our website

Our website is a simple, static site. It does not set any first-party cookies and does not use analytics or advertising cookies. The only third-party resource loaded by the website is the web font served by Google Fonts, which may receive your IP address as part of the font request. Please refer to Google’s privacy policy for details.

Because we do not use non-essential cookies on the website, no cookie consent banner is shown. If this changes in the future, we will update this page and ask for your consent where required by law.

Identifiers and similar technologies in our games

Our games do not use browser cookies, but they do use third-party software development kits (SDKs) that may collect device identifiers and usage data for analytics, attribution and advertising purposes. The partners we work with are:

  • GameAnalytics
  • Facebook Analytics
  • Adjust
  • Voodoo

Details about the data they collect, the purposes and how to request deletion can be found in our Privacy Policy.

Your choices

You can control cookies through your browser settings, including blocking or deleting them. On mobile devices, you can limit ad tracking or reset your advertising identifier in your device settings: on iOS under Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking, and on Android under Settings → Google → Ads.

Changes to this policy

We may update this Cookies Policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with a new “Last updated” date.
